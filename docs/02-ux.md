# Yoga Center Attendance Management App — UX

## Purpose
UX source of truth after Product Requirements. Defines Information Architecture, roles, key user flows, UX relationships, important UX patterns, and Figma planning. Product requirements remain in `01-product.md`.

`01-product.md` is the product source of truth. This document translates those requirements into UX structure and flows. If a product requirement changes, this document must be updated accordingly.

### Student Details
```text
Student Details (single page)
├── Profile / Contact
├── Membership
├── Batch Enrollments
├── Recent Attendance
└── Communication History
```

**Student Details is a single page, not tabs.** The areas above are
panels on one page, as shown in the approved wireframe. Do not introduce tab
navigation for Student Details. Batch Details, by contrast, does use tabs —
the two screens differ deliberately.

Top to bottom: the compact page strip; the identity header (name, status,
Student ID, gender, join date, and the actions); four compact summary cards
(Enrollments, Membership, Status, Joined); then the panels in two columns on
wide screens: Batch Enrollments and Recent Attendance on the left, Current
Membership and Profile & Contact (phone, email, date of birth, gender) on the
right; and Additional Information (the notes, or a compact "No additional
information yet") full width at the bottom. On smaller screens the panels
stack in that working order (Batch Enrollments, Membership, Profile & Contact,
Recent Attendance, Additional Information).

**Communication History** (`01-product.md` §4 "Communication History") is a full-width panel directly above Additional Information:
- **Add Communication** opens a dialog (the form-dialog pattern, with the student's context card): Date & Time (defaults to now on the centre's clock; can be moved back, not forward), Communication Type (Phone Call, WhatsApp, In Person, Other), Subject, Communication Notes, and an **Add Follow-up** switch. Switched on, it reveals Follow-up Date (not before the communication date) and Follow-up Note, both required; switched off, they are hidden and not saved. Errors appear on the fields.
- **Each record**, newest first: subject, a type badge, date and time, "Recorded by" (an Instructor is marked "(Instructor)"), the notes, and - when it has one - a follow-up box with the due date, the note and a **Pending** or **Completed** badge (a completed one also says when and by whom).
- **Edit** - a subtle pencil on each record opens the same form, titled **Edit Communication** with **Save Changes**, filled with the record's current values. A completed follow-up is shown but cannot be changed or switched off, with a note explaining that it records what was done. The timeline itself does not change.
- **Mark as Completed** on a pending follow-up opens a short confirmation; nothing else changes and no communication is added.
- **Empty state:** "No communication records have been added for this student yet." with Add Communication. If the history cannot be loaded, the panel says so and the rest of the page still works.

The **Batch Enrollments** panel shows each enrollment's assigned schedules
alongside the batch, identified by weekday and time. Without them the panel
cannot answer the question an admin actually brings to it — why a student
does or does not appear in a particular class session — since a batch may
run several schedules and the student attends only the ones they are
assigned to. An enrollment with no current assignment is shown as such
rather than left blank, because it is a configuration mistake worth seeing
(§4 of `01-product.md`).

### Settings
```text

├── Center Profile
├── Invoice / Receipt

├── Instructors

```

**Invoice / Receipt** (V1 Invoice / Receipt Enhancement) is the second Settings tab, between Center Profile and
Instructors: the configuration for newly issued invoices — document title, starting invoice number, tax, terms &
conditions, signatory and signature. *(GSTIN.)* The Tax panel also holds an optional **GSTIN** field, shown in the business details of new tax invoices (not on payment receipts). It follows the Center Profile screen's form pattern. Business identity (name,
address, phone, email, logo) stays in Center Profile. The starting invoice number is locked once an invoice has
been issued, and settings apply to invoices issued afterwards only. *(V1 Tax Adjustment.)* The non-tax payment
receipt has its own numbering sequence, separate from the tax invoice sequence (01-product.md §5A, §11).

*(V1 Fee Structure.)* **Packages and fees** (01-product.md §5, §11) are also configured by the Admin in Settings: packages (name, description, type), duration options with a monthly base fee and per-option package discounts, class-count allowance and price, the effective-dated fee history with scheduled entries (schedule, edit or cancel before effective; correct afterwards with a reason), activate / deactivate, and the audit history of package and fee changes. A blocked fee-history change shows the affected memberships and their agreed fees. Instructors have no access. These screens are the **Packages & Fees** tab, between Invoice / Receipt and Instructors (Roles & Permissions stays last): a list of packages (name, type, what it offers, the fee in effect, status) with Add Package, and a package page with its details, terms (duration options or class allowance), fee history (schedule, edit a scheduled fee, cancel, correct a fee in effect with a reason), activate / deactivate and the audit history. Layout follows the existing Settings screens (Instructors list, Panels with a section header and action); approved for implementation without a separate wireframe. The package-linked Membership Details actions and the Custom Fee Plan flow (§ "Membership fee, discounts and class-count packages") and the affected-memberships view still **need wireframe approval**.

**Reset Password** is a card at the bottom of the Roles & Permissions tab (Roles & Permissions remains the last of the four Settings tabs):
the signed-in user changes their **own** password with Current Password, New Password and Confirm New Password
(each with a show / hide control) and one Reset Password action. The rule is the project's existing one: at least
8 characters, and the two new entries must match. The current password is verified with Supabase Auth before
anything changes; a wrong one is reported on that field and nothing is changed. It stays on the page: success is
the standard toast and the three fields empty; errors appear beside the field or in a banner, never with
technical detail. It cannot change anyone else's password, and passwords are never stored or logged. Settings is
Admin-only, so today only an Admin reaches this card; an Instructor who needs a new password uses "Forgot
password?" on the sign-in page.

## Core UX Relationships

```text
Student
   │
   ├───────────────┐
   ↓               ↓
Membership      Enrollment
                   │
             ┌─────┴─────┐
             ↓           ↓
          Batch A      Batch B
             │           │
             ↓           ↓
      Schedule Assignment(s)
             │
             ↓
        Schedule Series
             ↓
       Schedule (version)
             ↓
        Class Session
             ↓
         Attendance
```

A batch may run several schedules, including more than one on the same weekday. An enrollment records which of them the student attends, so the path from a student to a class session runs through their schedule assignments — not through the batch alone.

Attendance eligibility:

```text
Active Student
          +
Active Batch Enrollment on Session Date
          +
Schedule Assignment on Session Date
   for the session's schedule series
          +
Active Membership on Session Date
          ↓
Eligible for Attendance
```

Membership alone does not make a student eligible for a particular batch, and batch enrollment alone does not make a student eligible for a particular class: a student assigned only to the 6:00 AM class is not eligible for the same batch's 7:00 PM class.

Historical attendance remains associated with the original class session even when membership, enrollment, batch, or schedule information changes later.

### 03 — Admin: Create Batch + Schedule
```text
Batches
  ↓
Add Batch
  ↓
Batch Details
  ↓
Save
  ↓
New Batch → Schedules
  ↓
Add Schedule
  ↓
Select Day(s) — one or more weekdays (checkboxes); a schedule is created for each
  ↓
Start Time (hour / minute / AM-PM dropdowns)
  ↓
End Time automatically +1 hour
  ↓
Edit End Time if needed
  ↓
Select Instructor
  ↓
Capacity — optional (blank = no limit)
  ↓
Effective From
  ↓
Save
  ↓
Schedule Active
```

Each schedule created for a ticked day gets the capacity entered (01-product.md §7 "Schedule Capacity").

**Instructor conflict** (01-product.md §7 "Conflicts"). Save is refused when any schedule the form would create gives the instructor two classes at overlapping times on the same day during overlapping dates — against an existing schedule, or between two of this form's own day and time slots. Back-to-back classes are not a conflict. Nothing is saved; the error names the conflicting schedule (batch, day, time and dates) and says to change the time, day, instructor or dates. Everything entered stays in the form.

### 04 — Admin: Change Recurring Schedule
```text
Schedule
 ↓
Weekly Schedule
 ↓
Select Schedule
 ↓
Edit
 ↓
Change Time / Instructor / Details
 ↓
Set Effective From
 ↓
Review Change
 ↓
Confirm
 ↓
Save
```

Review must explain that future sessions change while past sessions and attendance remain unaffected.

Edit shows the same weekday checkboxes as Add. The schedule's own day is pre-ticked and locked (a schedule is one recurring class with its own student assignments); ticking other days also creates a new schedule on each of them with the same instructor, times and dates, listed in the review as "Also creates". Each also created schedule gets the capacity being saved by this form.

**Capacity** (01-product.md §7 "Schedule Capacity") is edited on the same form but is not an effective-dated change: a new capacity takes effect as soon as the form is saved, creates no new version and needs no effective date — even when the same save changes the time or instructor from a future Effective From. When the capacity changes, Review states it separately from the dated changes ("Capacity: 20 → 25, from now") so it is not read as starting on the Effective From date. Lowering it below the places taken now is refused on Save, with the number of places taken.

**Unused schedule (01-product.md §7 "Unused Schedules").** When the schedule has no class session, a single version and no started assignment, Edit changes it in place: the day is not locked (untick the current day and tick another to move it), Effective From / Until are prefilled with the schedule's own dates, and Review says the changes apply directly with no new version. If students hold unstarted assignments on it, the form and Review say how many and that a day change moves their assignment. A schedule a direct edit creates on another ticked day gets the capacity being saved by this form, and a capacity change applies immediately, as above.

**Instructor conflict** (01-product.md §7 "Conflicts"). Save is refused, as on Add Schedule, when the edited schedule (a new version or a direct edit) or any schedule it also creates would conflict with another schedule of the same instructor. Nothing is saved, the error names the conflicting schedule, and everything entered stays in the form. Deactivating a schedule or setting an earlier Effective Until is never refused for a conflict.

### 05 — Admin: Deactivate Recurring Schedule
```text
Schedule
 ↓
Select Schedule
 ↓
Deactivate
 ↓
Set Effective Until / End Date
 ↓
Review Change
 ↓
Confirm
 ↓
Save
 ↓
Schedule Inactive
```

Deactivation must not delete historical sessions or attendance. Past records remain available.

The Delete Schedule dialog (01-product.md §7 "Unused Schedules", revised in 0063) says what will happen: for the **latest of several versions** that only this version is deleted and the earlier versions and the students' assignments remain, with a warning that the class will not continue beyond the previous version's end date; for a class's only version, how many students' unstarted assignments are withdrawn; and, in every case, how many past occurrences were never recorded. When deletion is refused it names the reason: a class session of this version, a later version (delete it first), or - for a class's only version - a student assignment that has started.

### 05A — Admin: Activate Inactive Schedule
```text
Schedule Details (inactive schedule)
 ↓
Activate Schedule
 ↓
Confirm Effective From (defaults to today, can be changed)
 ↓
Activate
 ↓
New version of the class, active from that date
```

Activate Schedule replaces Deactivate Schedule on Schedule Details while the schedule is inactive (01-product.md §7 "Activation"). The dialog asks only for Effective From, defaulting to today on the centre's clock; cancelling it changes nothing. A refusal - the date is in the past or not after the schedule's end date, the class is already active on that date, or the instructor would have two classes at overlapping times - is shown inside the dialog, which stays open so the date can be changed. On success the Admin lands on the new version's Schedule Details. Edit Schedule on an inactive schedule explains that it must be activated first, and offers only the capacity.

Deactivation does not end the assignments of the students who attend the schedule (01-product.md §7 "Deactivation"); a deactivated schedule is no longer offered when selecting schedules. Ending those assignments automatically is a separate, future decision.

### 05A — Admin: Delete Unused Schedule
```text
Schedule / Batch Details → Schedules
 ↓
Schedule action menu → Delete Schedule
 ↓
Server checks the schedule
 ↓
Delete Schedule? — Review (schedule, students affected, unrecorded past occurrences)
 ↓
Confirm
 ↓
Schedule deleted
```

Delete Schedule is the last item of the schedule action menu, below a divider. The dialog names the schedule (day · time), states that it has no recorded sessions or attendance, and says it cannot be undone. If students hold unstarted assignments it says how many and that those assignments are removed; if past occurrences were never recorded it says how many and that they will no longer appear.

If the schedule has a class session (any status), an earlier version, or a started student assignment, Delete is not offered: the dialog reads "Schedule can't be deleted", explains that its history must be preserved, and links to Schedule Details, where Deactivate is available. If the schedule becomes used while the dialog is open, confirming shows this same message instead of deleting.

#### Schedule screens
- The Schedule area has **two views**, switched by a toggle beneath the page
  heading: **Weekly Schedule** and **List View**. Both are approved and drawn
  in the wireframe, and both show the same recurring schedule records — the
  page heading, description and Add Schedule action stay the same across
  them. ("Weekly Schedule List View" in an extracted text rendering of the
  wireframe is those two toggle labels sitting side by side, not one label
  for the list.)
- **Weekly Schedule is the default view.** Opening the Schedule area with no
  view selected shows the week grid, as the Information Architecture above
  states ("Weekly Schedule ← Default"); List View is reached through the
  toggle. Phase 13 shipped with the List View as the default, which
  contradicts the approved IA; correcting that default is a Phase 13
  follow-up and changes nothing else about either view.
- The **Weekly Schedule** view is a week grid: a Today action, week-by-week
  navigation, the current week's date range, Monday–Sunday columns headed by
  their dates, and an hour-by-hour time axis down the side. Each recurring
  schedule appears as a card placed at its weekday and start time, showing
  the batch short code, the time range, and the instructor.
- The Weekly Schedule view **visualises recurring schedule records only**. It
  reads the same schedules the List View reads and lays them out by weekday
  and time. It does not create, store or modify class sessions, and it
  carries no session status, attendance, or exception behaviour — those
  belong to later phases.
- The **List View** is the tabular view: Batch, Day, Time, Instructor,
  Effective From, Effective Until, Status and Action columns, with the
  Batch / Instructor / Status filters, search, and pagination.
- **Schedule Details** is one page with no tabs (refined from the earlier Overview / Recurring Pattern /
  Upcoming Sessions tabs, whose panels repeated each other), in the same shape as Session Details: the compact
  page strip with a back button; the header card about WHEN the class happens - the start-time tile, "Every
  Sunday · 6:00 AM – 7:00 AM" as the heading (day and start time large, end time small) with the batch code
  and the schedule's status beside it, and under it the batch and instructor, with Edit Schedule and
  Deactivate Schedule at the right; four summary cards that add information (Frequency, Duration, Effective
  From with its end or "Open-ended", and the Next Session); then the Upcoming Sessions list and, beside it, the
  Recurring Pattern card - a Monday–Sunday strip with the schedule's day filled, the plain sentence, and the
  note that changes affect future sessions only.
- Schedule Details' **Upcoming Sessions** is a read-only projection of
  the recurring pattern across its effective period. It displays future
  occurrences; it does not create, store, or modify them. Each row's Edit Session action opens the existing
  Edit This Session page for that one date.
- Batch Details' **Schedules** tab lists the recurring schedules for that one
  batch and offers Add Schedule, as drawn in the wireframe.
- Batch Details has **four tabs: Overview, Students, Schedules, Attendance**
  (updated from the three drawn in the wireframe, by explicit product
  decision). The **Attendance** tab is Attendance History scoped to that one
  batch: the same search, filters (without the Batch filter, since the batch
  is fixed), sort, Cards / Table layouts and pagination. Overview shows a **monthly Attendance strip**
  (below) that links to the tab.
- Batch Details' **Overview** is a **full-width, single-column stack** (it replaces the earlier Weekly Schedule beside a Students preview, with
  a Recent Attendance preview below): the header card and the four tabs; four short summary cards; the **Weekly Schedule**; the **Students**
  section; and the **Attendance** strip, each section across the whole width. The batch's description is shown in the header, so there is no
  separate Additional Information panel. The Schedules tab keeps the full table with dates and actions.
  - **Summary cards** (short values, so none wraps; one column on a phone, four from the extra-wide breakpoint): **Active Students** (the
    number of active enrollments, caption "Active enrollments" - 01-product.md §6 "Active Students"), **Weekly Classes** (the number of
    current schedules, caption "Across N days"), **Time**, and **Instructor** - the instructor's photo and name when every current class has
    the same instructor, otherwise the summary text.
  - **Weekly Schedule**: a panel with Add Schedule and View Full Schedule. Each weekday, Monday to Sunday, is its own card in a grid whose columns
    are as wide as a time range needs and as many as the panel's own width allows - up to seven across, fewer (several rows) when it is
    narrower, so a time is never clipped. A day with no class says "No class". Only the batch's **current** schedules are shown. A class shows
    its time range as a coloured chip - the same time range has the same colour on every day, the same mapping the enrollment schedule picker
    uses, and the time is always written out, never left to the colour - then, when the batch's classes are not all taught by one person,
    the instructor's name, then the class's own places (capacity, 01-product.md §7 "Schedule Capacity").
  - **Students**: one collapsible panel with a single table of the batch's enrollments. Columns: #, Student (photo and name, a link to Student
    Details), Status (the enrollment's own Active / Inactive), Student ID and Phone (Student ID is hidden on a phone, Phone below tablet width),
    and Actions. **All enrollments are shown by default**, active and inactive. A **search** box (name, Student ID or phone) and a status
    filter (**All Status**, **Active**, **Inactive**) narrow the rows on screen only; neither changes the count in the heading, which is the
    **Active Students** number the summary card shows (so the two always agree). A chevron at the right of the header collapses the panel;
    collapsing only hides the table, so the search and the filter are still there when it is expanded. With no enrollments the panel says no
    students are enrolled; with none matching it says so and offers Clear Filters. **Actions** per student: a View button (Student Details) and
    a menu with **View Student** and **Edit Enrollment** (the existing Edit Batch Enrollment page, Flow 10). **Add Student** in the header opens
    the existing **Add Student** guided flow (Flow 02) at Student Details. It does **not** enroll anyone in this batch: the batch is not passed
    on, and a new student is enrolled in a batch only at that flow's own Batch Enrollment step, or later from Student Details.
  - **Attendance strip**: one compact card per day of the **month shown**, in a single horizontal row that scrolls inside its own box (the page
    never scrolls sideways) with previous / next scroll buttons; the card for today - or, in a past month, the month's last day - is highlighted
    and scrolled into view. The month's header has previous / next month links (the next-month link is disabled in the current month - there is
    no attendance in the future), the month name, a legend (Present, Absent, No Class) and **View All Attendance**, which opens the Attendance
    tab. The month is part of the address (`?attendanceMonth=`). The current month shows days up to today; a past month shows every day.
    Each card opens the Attendance tab filtered to that one date. A day is exactly one of:
    - **Recorded** - the batch had one or more completed sessions that day: the **Present** count (green) over the **Absent** count (red),
      summed when there were several sessions. A completed class with nobody present or absent is still a recorded day showing zeros.
    - **No record** - a schedule of this batch falls on that weekday within its effective dates, but no completed session exists: attendance was
      not marked, or the session was cancelled or a holiday. It is never shown as zero attendance.
    - **No Class** - nothing of this batch is scheduled that day; no counts are shown.
    If the month cannot be loaded the strip says so and invents no counts.
- **Add Schedule, Edit Schedule, and Deactivate Schedule have no dedicated
  approved wireframes.** They are composed from already-approved UX patterns —
  the Students/Batches form layout, and Review → Confirm → Save for edit and
  deactivation (Flows 04 and 05) — rather than derived from a new wireframe
  screen. This mirrors the composed Membership screens noted above.

#### Weekly Schedule view — week navigation
The wireframe draws a Today action and the current week's date range. The
navigation controls that make them meaningful are settled here:

- A **Today** button returns the grid to the week containing the current date.
- A **Previous week** and a **Next week** button move the grid one week at a
  time.
- The **current week's date range** is displayed alongside them.
- Navigation changes **only which week is displayed**. It loads no new kind of
  record and changes nothing stored.
- There is no month view and no day view.

#### Weekly Schedule view — time axis
The wireframe draws a partial time axis only (its sample runs from 6 AM to
early afternoon), which is not enough to implement against. The following is
an **explicit decision resolving that ambiguity**, not a reading of the
wireframe:

- The grid begins at **6:00 AM** and extends through **10:00 PM**.
- Time labels are at **one-hour** intervals.
- The range is **fixed**. It must not expand, contract or shift based on which
  schedules happen to exist.
- **Vertical scrolling** is allowed when the full range does not fit the
  viewport.
- Each schedule card is positioned by its **weekday and its start and end
  time**.

#### Weekly Schedule view — overlapping schedules
V1 permits overlapping schedules for a batch when they have different
instructors, and keeps instructor conflicts saved before that rule was added
(`01-product.md` §7 "Conflicts"), so two cards can occupy the same weekday and
time. The wireframe draws no such case. The following is an **explicit
decision resolving that ambiguity**, not a reading of the wireframe:

- Overlapping schedules **remain visible**. None is hidden, collapsed or
  summarised, and there is no "+N more" affordance.
- Overlapping cards are rendered **side by side** within the same weekday and
  time area.
- The grid is **display-only**: no drag-to-reschedule, and no resizing a card
  to change its time.
- The grid performs **no conflict prevention or warning** of its own. An
  instructor conflict is refused when a schedule is saved (Add Schedule and
  Edit, below); showing an overlap is not the same as blocking one.

#### Session-level flows belong to a later phase
Flows 06 (Change One Specific Session) and 07 (Cancel / Holiday) both start
from the Schedule area, but they act on an individual class session rather
than on a recurring schedule. Class Sessions are a separate phase from
Schedule (see `04-development-plan.md` §5), so these two flows are delivered
with Class Sessions, not with the Schedule screens above.

Both flows also **materialize** the session they act on: an occurrence that
was until then only projected from the recurring pattern becomes a stored
record at that moment, snapshotting its batch, instructor, date and time
(`01-product.md` §7A). Neither flow has a dedicated approved wireframe, so
both are composed from already-approved patterns — the existing form layout
plus Review → Confirm → Save, exactly as the Membership and Schedule screens
were.

#### Class Session screens
The class-session surface lives under the **Attendance** navigation item, as
the Information Architecture above sets out — not under Schedule:

```text
Attendance
├── Today's Sessions      ← Default
├── All Sessions
├── Class Count Attendance ← third tab, Admin and Instructor: record the classes of class-count memberships (an Instructor: their own students; reversing is Admin only)
├── Session Details       ← "View Session Details": information, summary, eligible students
└── Take Attendance       ← the marking screen (its own page, no tabs)
```

- **Today's Sessions** is the default view: today's date in the heading, and
  a row per session with Time, Batch, Instructor and Status.
- **All Sessions** adds Date and its own filters — search by batch or
  instructor, a date range, Batch, Instructor and Session Status — plus
  pagination.
- **Session Details** ("View Session Details" in a list row's menu) is one page with no tabs: the compact

A student may belong to multiple batches simultaneously. Enrollment changes must preserve historical attendance.

#### Start date and join date
- The Effective Start Date field says it cannot be before the student's join date (shown as a date). A start date before it is a field error; the same rule applies on the membership forms' Start Date.
- Editing the start date of an existing enrollment moves its initial schedule assignments with it. The confirmation message after saving says how many moved ("N schedule assignments were moved to the new start date."); it is not shown when none moved. A start date that would overlap or contradict another assignment is refused with a message on the field, and nothing is saved.
- Edit Student refuses a join date later than the start of an active enrollment or a non-cancelled membership, naming how many there are and the earliest start; the Admin corrects those first or moves the join date earlier.

#### Recorded attendance on the effective date
- A start date (new enrollment or changed) or a Schedule Change Effective Date on which the student already has recorded class-session attendance, in any batch, is refused on Save with a field error: "This student already has attendance recorded on {date}. Choose a later effective date." Nothing is saved. End date, status and class-count attendance are not checked.

#### Selecting schedules
- Schedule selection appears **after** batch selection and lists only the schedules of the chosen batch that are currently in effect. Changing the batch replaces the list and clears any prior selection, since a schedule belongs to one batch.
- Each option identifies the class the way the admin recognises it — weekday, start and end time, and instructor — so several same-weekday schedules of one batch are told apart by their times rather than by an internal identifier.
- **Multiple selection is supported.** A student attending both the 6:00 AM and the 7:00 PM class of a batch is one enrollment with two schedule assignments, not two enrollments.
- **At least one schedule must be selected.** Saving with none is a validation error on the schedule field, not a silent save — an enrollment with no assignments makes the student eligible for nothing.
- Schedules whose times overlap may both be selected. V1 treats that as the admin's operational judgement (01-product.md §7 "Conflicts": only an instructor's own schedules may not overlap).

#### Schedule capacity *(V1 Final Improvements — 01-product.md §7 "Schedule Capacity")*
- The flow is: select a batch, select one or more of its schedules, and save; on Save, the capacity of **each** selected schedule is checked. Each selected schedule takes one place in its own schedule.
- Each schedule option shows its places — places taken and capacity, or that it has no limit — and a **full** schedule is shown as full. When the enrollment will take places once saved (it is active and has no end date, or ends today or later), a full schedule cannot be newly selected. When editing a current enrollment, a schedule it already attends (including one that starts later) stays selected and selectable even if that schedule is now full, since keeping it takes no new place. The exception is an edit that reactivates an inactive enrollment or extends an ended one: its schedules then take places again, so a full one is refused like a newly selected schedule.
- An enrollment that stays inactive or ended takes no places (01-product.md §7 "Schedule Capacity"), so the picker does not lock full schedules for it: they are marked Full but can still be selected, and the save is not refused for capacity. When it is later reactivated, or its end date extended, so that it takes places again, the database enforces capacity at that save.
- What the form shows is a guide; the database decides on Save. **The save is all-or-nothing:** if any selected schedule is full at that moment (for example another Admin took the last place), nothing is saved — not the enrollment, not its other schedules, and no existing assignment is ended — and the schedule field says which schedule is full and that its capacity can be raised first.
- A future-dated enrollment or schedule change already takes its place, and a schedule change effective on a future date counts toward both the old and the new schedule until the old assignment's end date has passed. The form explains this when it is the reason a schedule is full.

#### Changing schedule assignments
Changing which schedules a student attends is dated, not retroactive. The admin sets the date the change takes effect; the existing assignment is closed the day before and a new assignment starts from that date. Past sessions keep resolving against the assignment that was in effect then. A newly added schedule must have a free place (see "Schedule capacity" above); ending a schedule is never refused because of capacity.

The Review step must state plainly which schedules are being added and which are being ended, and from which date — the same Review → Confirm → Save gate the rest of this flow already uses. It must not imply that historical attendance or past eligibility changes, because neither does.

#### States
- **No schedules available** — the chosen batch has no schedules currently in effect. Say so directly and point the admin at adding a schedule for that batch first, rather than showing an empty control with no explanation. Saving is blocked, because there is nothing to assign.
- **No schedule selected** — validation error on the field, described above.
- **Every schedule full** — the chosen batch has schedules in effect but none with a free place. Say so, rather than showing only disabled options, and point the admin at raising a schedule's capacity first.
- **Schedule became full on Save** — a field error on the schedule field naming the full schedule; nothing is saved, described above.
- **Assignment unchanged** — an edit that changes no schedules performs no assignment write, and says nothing changed, matching how the session-level flows already treat a no-op save.

**Class Attendance panel (Membership Details).** For a class-count membership only, a history panel lists every record of the membership: the attendance date, when and by whom it was recorded, and its status — **Recorded**, or **Reversed** with the reversal's date, person and reason. Each Recorded row has **Reverse**; there is no edit and no delete. The panel summarises the classes used and left and says "No classes recorded yet." when there are none. *(Expiry edits.)* After the expiry is shortened, a Recorded row whose attendance date is after the current expiry is kept as it is and shown with the informational flag **Attendance after current expiry**; it is not reversed or hidden, the class balance is unchanged, and there is no acknowledgement step in V1. New classes can only be recorded within the current validity. It is read like the other Membership Details panels and shows nothing for a duration membership.

**Class-count attendance on Student Details and Attendance History** *(Student Details is Admin only; Attendance History is shown to an Admin and, limited to their own students, to an Instructor).*
- **Student Details → Recent Attendance** is one list of at most 5 rows, newest first, with a **Type** column: **Session** (batch, Present / Absent, instructor, as before) or **Class count** (the membership code, a link to Membership Details, instead of a batch; Recorded or Reversed; no instructor). A Reversed row stays in the list, muted, and is not a class used. **Attendance after current expiry** appears only on a Recorded row after the current expiry. If only one kind of attendance can be loaded the other is shown with a short note; "not available" appears only when neither can.
- **Attendance History:** under each date's sessions a separate **Class count** section lists that date's records — student, membership (link), and Recorded or Reversed with its reason, plus the expiry flag where it applies — as cards or a table like the sessions. The date header keeps its "N Sessions" count and adds "· N Class count" after it; a date with only class-count records shows just "N Class count". Those dates are also marked on the calendar and listed in Quick dates (labelled "N class count" beside any session count). A batch, instructor or Present / Absent filter hides the section and a short note says so; there is no Type filter. Search matches the student name or membership code.
- **Instructor view of Attendance History:** the same section, calendar markers and Quick dates, limited to the students that instructor is authorized for (an active enrollment with a current assignment on a series they teach, or attendance on a session they own — 01-product.md §8). A Reversed record shows the **Reversed** badge only: no reason, no reversal time, and no link to Membership Details. The Instructor's session table, its Status column and its session totals are unchanged.
- If the class-count records cannot be loaded the sessions still show, with a short note.

#### Membership Receipt
Each membership's overflow menu (Memberships list cards and table, and the
Membership History table) has **View Receipt**, which opens
`/memberships/[id]/receipt` — a printable receipt generated from the
membership record, the student and the Center Profile; nothing is stored. The
membership ID is the receipt number, the membership's creation date is the
receipt date, and the total is the membership's amount. *(V1 Fee Structure.)* The receipt layout is unchanged for every membership: it shows the total only, with no fee breakdown; the breakdown is shown on Membership Details. The schema has no
payment-method or bank fields, so none are shown. **Print Receipt**
uses the browser's print dialog; the application navigation, the back link and
the buttons are hidden in print, and the page is set for A4. **Send WhatsApp**
opens a `wa.me` click-to-chat link to the student's registered phone with the
receipt details pre-filled; it is a plain link the admin sends themselves, not a
WhatsApp integration (those stay out of V1), and it is disabled when the student
has no usable mobile number.

#### Membership & payment reminders
*(V1 Reminders; 01-product.md §5 "Reminders".)* Admin-only and in-app; worked out each time from current data, so what is shown always matches the membership. Nothing is sent to anyone, and no wording on these screens suggests that a message was sent. There is no separate Reminders page, and a reminder cannot be dismissed or snoozed in V1.

- **Dashboard → Reminders panel.** A panel on the Admin Dashboard lists the current reminders: for each one the student (name, photo, Student ID), the membership (code and plan or package) and either the expiry (days left, "Expires today", or days overdue, with the end date) or the **outstanding balance**. Each row links to that membership's Membership Details. A membership with both reminders is listed once with both facts. Empty state: "No reminders - every membership is current and paid up." If the reminders cannot be loaded, the panel says so and the rest of the Dashboard still works. An Instructor's Dashboard has no Reminders panel.
- **Memberships list → reminder indicators (Table rows and Cards).** In both layouts each membership carries a compact indicator for each reminder it has, next to the existing status and payment-status badges, without widening the row or the card:
  - **Expiry** - red (the danger tone): "Expires in N days", "Expires today" or "Expired N days ago".
  - **Payment** - a payment-pending indicator with the outstanding balance (for example "Balance ₹1,500").
  When both apply both are shown, compact, and wrap rather than crowd the row or card; when a condition no longer applies its indicator is not shown.
- **Memberships list → More (⋮) menu.** For a membership with a reminder the menu states it at the top ("Expires in 3 days", "Balance due ₹1,500") and offers the action that resolves it, using the existing destinations: **Renew Membership** (Flow 09) for an expiry reminder and **Record Payment** for a payment reminder, together with View Membership. Record Payment opens Membership Details, where payments are already recorded; the list has no payment-entry form of its own. These are in-app actions only.
- **Notification bell.** The existing Notifications bell (sidebar and mobile menu) shows the total number of current reminders, counting each reminder separately - a membership with both an expiry and a payment reminder counts 2 - and no count when there are none. The count is Admin-only. Selecting the bell opens the Dashboard's Reminders panel. In V1 it is the only use of the bell.

## Important UX Patterns

### Status Separation
Keep these concepts independent in the UX:

- **Attendance:** Present / Absent
- **Membership:** Upcoming / Active / Expired / Cancelled
- **Payment:** Pending / Partially Paid / Paid
- **Class Session:** Upcoming / Ongoing / Completed / Cancelled / Holiday

Do not use payment status as a substitute for membership status, attendance status, or class-session status.



### Review Before Important Changes
Use:
```text
Edit → Review → Confirm → Save
```
for recurring schedule changes, one-session exceptions, cancellation/holiday, and attendance corrections.

### Say Which Record a Popup Is About
Every popup that acts on a record (review, confirm, deactivate, cancel, delete, mark) opens with a
context card directly under its title and description, so the person always knows where they are
and what the question below will change. The card shows the record's own mark, its name, one detail
line and, where useful, one more line:
- **Student** (deactivate, review enrollment, review membership, cancel membership): photo, name,
  Student ID, and for a membership popup its code, plan and period.
- **Schedule** (deactivate, delete, review a schedule change, view a schedule): the batch's mark, name and
  code, the day and time, and the instructor.
- **Session** (mark cancelled / holiday, review attendance, review a session change): the start-time
  tile, the batch and its code, the date and time, and the instructor.

One generic card (`ContextCard`) backs all three, so popups about the same kind of record read alike.

### Protect Historical Data
Make it clear when a change affects future records only. Past attendance must not be silently changed by:
- membership expiry or renewal
- student batch changes or leaving a batch
- student deactivation
- batch deactivation
- recurring schedule changes
- future session changes or cancellation.

### Fast Attendance
Primary instructor path:
```text
Dashboard → Today's Class → Take Attendance
```

### Avoid Duplication
Use contextual entry points without creating duplicate concepts:
- Student attendance from Student Details
- Batch schedules from Batch Details
- Session attendance from Attendance
- Historical records from Attendance History
- Summaries from Reports

### Global Shell: User Identity Placement

Approved implementation decision for the global application shell:

- The signed-in user's identity (name + role) appears in the **global Header**,
  in the top-right account menu.
- The **Sidebar footer contains Logout only.**
- Do **not** duplicate the user profile in the Sidebar.

Some wireframe screens show the user's name and role repeated at the bottom of
the Sidebar alongside Logout. That duplication is superseded by the decision
above. Do not reintroduce it when implementing or extending the shell.

### Login: visual-reference caution

`docs/ui-reference/login.png` is a visual/composition reference only. It shows
**Remember me**, **Google Sign-In** and a **Light/Dark theme toggle**, none of
which are approved V1 functionality in `01-product.md` or this document.

Do not implement those elements from the reference image. See the "Known
reference conflict: `login.png`" section in `CLAUDE.md`.

The sign-in page is a single white card centred on a soft teal-white background with large translucent
leaves in two corners: the brand mark and "Yoga Center / Attendance System" above the card; inside it
"Welcome Back / Sign in to your account", Email address and Password (each with a leading icon, the
password with a show / hide control), "Forgot password?" under the password, a full-width Sign In with an
arrow, and "Need help? Contact your administrator."; the copyright line below the card. It is the same
email-and-password sign-in as before. An Admin / Instructor switch and Sign in with Google appeared in a
later mockup and are not built: a person's role comes from their account, so a switch has nothing to do, and
Google Sign-In is not approved. The other authentication screens (forgot / reset password, invitation and
recovery links) keep the split layout until they are moved to this one.

## UX Principles
1. Operational first — prioritize frequent admin/instructor actions.
2. Simple attendance — V1 is Present/Absent only.
3. Flexible scheduling — add, change, deactivate, or make one-time exceptions.
4. Historical integrity — future changes must not damage past records.
5. Clear relationships — Student, Membership, Enrollment, Schedule Assignment, Batch, Schedule, Session, Attendance remain distinct.
6. Guided setup — new student and batch setup guide the Admin through required steps.
7. Role clarity — Admin has full access; Instructor has focused access.
8. Don't overbuild — add complexity only for a real requirement.
